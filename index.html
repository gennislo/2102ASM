Syllables & Stress Breakdown Rules

Quick guide on how to pronounce broken down words

1. Syllable Separation ( · ) Dots indicate where the word naturally breaks down into sound units. Each syllable contains a vowel sound.
2. Capitalized Stress (CAPS) Capitalized syllables (e.g. ac-COM-mo-date) receive the primary stress, spoken louder and clearer.
3. Word Families Note how stress shifts when derivative forms are created (e.g. PROD-uct vs pro-DUC-tion).
